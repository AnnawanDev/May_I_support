# May I? Support

Thanks for playing May I? If something isn't working, you have an idea, or you just have a question, I'm happy to help.

## Get in touch

There are two ways to reach me:

- **Report a problem or suggest an idea on GitHub:** [open an issue](https://github.com/AnnawanDev/May_I_support/issues/new) in the [May I? support repository](https://github.com/AnnawanDev/May_I_support). This works best for bugs and feature ideas, and you can see whether someone else has already reported the same thing. You'll need a free GitHub account.
- **Send a direct message on X:** message [@May_I_gin_rummy](https://x.com/May_I_gin_rummy). This works well for quick questions.

## Reporting a bug

To help me fix a problem quickly, please include:

- **What happened,** and what you expected to happen instead
- **The steps that led to it,** if you can remember them (for example, "Round 3, I called May I? and then went down")
- **A screenshot or screen recording,** if you can
- **Your device and iOS version** (for example, iPhone 15 on iOS 18.2). You'll find the iOS version in **Settings → General → About**.
- **The app version.** Find it in **Settings → General → iPhone Storage → May I?**.

## Common questions

**How do I play?**
Tap **How to play** on the home screen for the full rules: the seven rounds, books and runs, going down, going out, and calling "May I?".

**Why can't I discard my last card?**
In May I?, going out means playing every card from your hand onto the table, without a discard. If your last card can't be played anywhere yet, you hold it and try again on your next turn.

**Why can't I take the discard pile?**
Once you've gone down, you can only draw from the draw pile. And if you take the discard by calling "May I?", you also take two penalty cards from the pile.

**Can I get a refund for a tip?**
Tips are processed by Apple, so refunds go through Apple too. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Does May I? collect my personal information?**
No. The app has no accounts or ads and sends only anonymous usage data. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md) for the details.

**My stats disappeared.**
Your stats are stored only on your device, so deleting and reinstalling the app resets them.
