# About May I?

**May I?** is the family rummy game many of us grew up playing around the kitchen table, now on iPhone and iPad. Build books and runs, race through seven rounds, and don't forget to ask the most important question in the game: *"May I?"*

## The game

May I? is a cousin of gin rummy, played with four decks and their jokers. Everyone is dealt 12 cards. On your turn you draw a card, then discard one. Each round has its own goal, and the first player to meet it, lay it down and play every card from their hand wins the round.

- A **book** is three or more cards of the same rank, in any suits (8♠ 8♥ 8♣).
- A **run** is four or more cards in a row, all of the same suit (2♥ 3♥ 4♥ 5♥).
- **Aces** can be low or high (A‑2‑3‑4 or J‑Q‑K‑A), but runs don't wrap around.
- **Jokers** stand in for any card.

### Seven rounds, seven goals

| Round | Goal |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 books |
| 2 | 1 book + 1 run |
| 3 | 2 runs |
| 4 | 3 books |
| 5 | 2 books + 1 run |
| 6 | 2 runs + 1 book |
| 7 | 3 runs |

### Going down and going out

When your hand meets the round's goal, you can **go down** by laying those cards on the table. After that you can only draw from the draw pile, but you can play cards onto anyone's books and runs. Play every card in your hand and you **go out**, winning the round. Sneaky players keep their cards hidden and go down and out all at once.

### "May I?"

See a card you want on the discard pile when it isn't your turn? If you haven't gone down yet, call **"May I?"** before the next player draws. The player whose turn it is decides. If they say yes, you get the card plus two penalty cards from the draw pile. If they say no, they have to take it themselves. You can ask twice per round.

### Scoring

Cards left in your hand at the end of a round count against you:

- Joker: 50
- Ace: 20
- King, Queen, Jack, 10: 10 each
- 2 through 9: face value

Cards you've laid down don't count. **The lowest total after seven rounds wins.**

## Meet the family

You play against two or three computer opponents, each with a personality of their own:

- **Grandma Dot** is sweet, but she never says yes to a "May I?"
- **Uncle Ray** goes down the moment he can.
- **Cousin Lou** is sneaky and holds out to go out all at once.

## Features

- A warm, retro card-table design made for landscape play
- A built-in **How to Play** guide covering every rule
- A round-by-round scoreboard and your personal stats
- A **gameplay speed** setting: Slow gives you more time to call "May I?", and Normal keeps things brisk
- **Table talk** from your opponents, which you can set to Quiet, Normal or Chatty in Settings
- Works offline, anytime

## No ads, no accounts

May I? has no ads and never asks who you are. It doesn't collect personal information. Read the [privacy policy](privacy.md) for the details. If you enjoy the game, you can leave an optional tip in Settings to support future updates.
