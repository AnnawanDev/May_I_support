# Privacy Policy for May I?

**Effective date:** November 13, 2026

May I? is a card game for iPhone and iPad made by Ed Wied ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what happens to it. The short version: May I? doesn't ask who you are, has no accounts or ads, and doesn't track you. It sends a small amount of anonymous usage data so I can see how the game is played and make it better.

## What May I? does not collect

- No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind
- No contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera access
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
- No selling or sharing of data with data brokers

## Information stored only on your device

The app keeps your game statistics (games played, games won, and your best score) on your device. This information is never sent to me or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Anonymous usage analytics

May I? uses [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com), a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand how the game is used, such as how often games are finished and which screens people open. TelemetryDeck processes this data on my behalf.

**What is sent:**

- **Gameplay events:** for example, that a game started (and with how many computer opponents), a round ended (the round number, its goal, whether you, a computer player, or nobody went out, and your points for the round), a game finished (won, lost, or tied, and your total score), that you went down, or that you called or answered "May I?".
- **Screens and taps:** that you opened How to Play, Stats, or Settings, or tapped the link to my X profile.
- **Technical details:** app version, device model and type, operating system version, screen size, orientation, language, region, time zone, light or dark mode, and accessibility display settings such as text size or reduced motion. These help me find bugs and make the game work well on every device.
- **An anonymous identifier:** your device's app-specific identifier is scrambled with a one-way hash (SHA-256) on your device before anything is sent. The original identifier never leaves your device, and the hashed value can't be turned back into it or linked to your identity. It lets me count how many people play without knowing who they are.

**What is not sent:** your name, contact details, the cards in your hand, or anything you type.

This data is not used to identify you, not combined with data from other apps, and not used for advertising. You can read more in [TelemetryDeck's privacy policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

## Tips (in-app purchases)

Settings has an optional tip jar. Tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details. When a tip is completed, the app records an anonymous analytics event with the tip amount and the App Store country, so I can see that tips are working. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Links to other sites

Settings includes a link to my profile on X (formerly Twitter). If you tap it, X's own privacy policy applies to anything you do there.

## Children's privacy

May I? is a family card game suitable for all ages. Because the app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

- **Delete your data:** deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.
- **Analytics:** usage data can't be linked back to you, so there's no profile of you to access or delete. If you have questions or concerns, contact me using the options below.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
